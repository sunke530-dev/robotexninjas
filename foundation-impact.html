STEM & Robotics Outreach
From Curiosity to Creation: Robotics Presentation & Workshop
September 12, 2026 • Little Elm Public Library
Who participated
Kids and parents from the community who attended the two-hour library session.
What participants did
Heard a short presentation on what makes a robot a robot and the team's VEX IQ journey, then built their own VEX creations, tried basic coding, and drove robots on a VEX field.
What our students contributed
Presented, taught, and facilitated the building, coding, and driving stations.
Materials or outcomes
Strong turnout; kids and parents stayed afterward with questions about robotics and the VEX program.

Vacation Bible School Robotics Days
July 8–10, 2026 • Collin County Chinese Fellowship Church
Who participated
4th–6th grade Vacation Bible School attendees.
What participants did
Learned robotics basics — drivetrains, chassis types, and bracing for structural strength — then built their own VEX IQ robots from a pile of beams and motors, finishing with a friendly driving competition.
What our students contributed
Led daily instruction and hands-on mentoring across the three-day track.
Materials or outcomes
Completed VEX IQ robots built by the kids; a driving competition to close the week.
