Our Purpose

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students turn curiosity into practical knowledge, creative work, and service to others. Through competitive robotics, independent-team mentorship, community STEM activities, and a developing Innovation Lab, we create opportunities to build, test, explain, and share ideas.

01 — Compete
🤖
We Learn Through Engineering and Competition

Our competitive robotics team — VEX IQ Team 276C, now progressing into V5RC as Team 91276C — builds skills in design, programming, testing, and teamwork. Competition gives that learning a proving ground: a place to evaluate designs, learn from other teams, and improve. We pursue competitive goals through student learning, teamwork, and iterative engineering. Results are one measure of progress—not the only one.

02 — Teach
🏠
We Bring STEM to the Community

The STEM Academy introduces robotics, coding, AI literacy, and engineering design through hands-on workshops and demonstrations at schools, libraries, and community spaces. Based in Frisco, serving North Texas communities. We're building toward a structured after-school program as well — details are announced as each offering launches.

03 — Invent
🔬
We Teach Students to Research, Design, and Protect Ideas

The Innovation Lab is being developed to support student researchers, science fair participants, and early-stage inventors — teaching how to investigate a problem, document an idea, and understand how intellectual property fits into the process. For eligible projects, we're also working to connect students with registered patent practitioners for pro bono guidance, subject to availability and a separate engagement.

04 — Grow
🌍
We Build Something That Outlasts Us

Every student we mentor and every workshop we run adds to a shared set of resources, know-how, and relationships that outlast any single season. We are building a continuing educational program that welcomes new participants and strengthens the wider STEM community.

Compete. — Teach. — Invent. — Grow.
How the Foundation Operates

The Foundation operates a continuing educational program for eligible students. Participating students’ learning and service advance that program; contributions and parental positions do not create ownership or preferential participation rights.

How Students Participate

Students and families may inquire about community activities, mentorship, and available competitive-team positions. Requirements vary by program and may include age, readiness, attendance, teamwork, and available instructional capacity.

Competitive-team places are limited. Current openings and selection requirements are published when recruitment is active. Voluntary contributions and parental leadership positions do not guarantee admission, renewal, or a particular role.

How Support Is Used

Financial and in-kind support helps provide shared robotics equipment, educational materials, mentoring, community activities, and appropriate competition experiences within the Foundation’s educational programs. Support may also cover reasonable program-administration costs.

Equipment acquired by the Foundation remains under its ownership and control for continued educational use. Required participation fees, where applicable, are identified separately from voluntary gifts.
