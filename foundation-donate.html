What does my gift support?

Gifts support the Foundation’s educational mission, including robotics equipment, learning materials, student engineering, mentorship, and community STEM programs. Explore our programs.

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

Ninjas STEM Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 42-2658141. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Contact us with questions about a particular contribution.

Can my business sponsor NSF?

Yes—contact us to discuss supporting our educational programs and any proposed recognition. View sponsorship information.

Who can I contact about giving?

Email contact@ninjasstemfoundation.com for Foundation gifts, sponsorships, or questions about a contribution.
