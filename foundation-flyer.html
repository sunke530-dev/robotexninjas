Ninjas STEM Foundation

EST. 2026 / FRISCO, TEXAS NINJAS STEM FOUNDATION FROM CURIOSITY TO CREATION A question today. A creation tomorrow. Student robotics, shared discoveries, and hands-on STEM learning for our North Texas community. LEARN / BUILD / SHARE

Curiosity into action

OUR PURPOSE CURIOSITY INTO ACTION Established in 2026, Ninjas STEM Foundation helps students turn curiosity into practical knowledge, creative work, and service. Through robotics, mentorship, and community learning, we build, test, explain, and share ideas. LITTLE ELM PUBLIC LIBRARY September 12, 2026 Students and families built VEX creations, explored basic coding, and drove robots with our student presenters. FOLLOW ROBOTEX NINJAS Our student team began in 2023. Scan for team history, season results, and robot videos. COMPETE. TEACH. INVENT. GROW.

Find your path

LEARN & BUILD FIND YOUR PATH COMPETITIVE ROBOTICS VEX IQ 276C TO V5RC 91276C Student-led engineering, coding, testing, documentation, and teamwork. See real design decisions and setbacks in our online engineering notebook. Our VEX IQ journey 3 World Championship appearances Back-to-back Division 3rd Place finishes 2x TX Region 5 Teamwork Champions 2x TX Region 5 Robot Skills Champions Regional titles: 2024-25 and 2025-26 seasons. ROBOTICS TEAM MENTORSHIP For developing VEX IQ and V5RC teams. Learn design, coding, and troubleshooting while students remain responsible for their own competition work. Ask us about mentorship for your team. MORE WAYS TO LEARN STEM Academy (in development) - structured, multi-session robotics, coding, engineering, and AI literacy; planned after-school learning for Grades 5-8. Innovation Lab (active) - research, science fairs, invention, and prototyping for Grades 5-12. Potential patent guidance is subject to practitioner availability and a separate engagement. BUILD SKILLS. TEST IDEAS. SHARE WHAT YOU LEARN.
