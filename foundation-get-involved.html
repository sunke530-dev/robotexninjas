Competitive Program: V5RC Team 91276C
Recruitment Status: Accepting Inquiries for Identified Roles
Eligibility and Preparation
Grades 5–8, consistent with our current roster. Prior robotics or VEX experience is helpful but not required. Specific roles carry their own expectations — for example, driving positions call for demonstrated practice time and reliable attendance, while build, CAD, and coding roles call for relevant hands-on or software interest and follow-through.
Commitment
Weekly team practices during the season, with additional practice time in the weeks ahead of competitions. Once a role is confirmed, members are expected to attend consistently and communicate ahead of any planned absence.
Review Process
Inquiries are reviewed by the coach and current team leadership on a rolling basis. A resume or activity summary (CV) is welcome but not required. Candidates who look like a possible fit are invited to a tryout, and we follow up directly by email either way.
Program Costs
A tryout is encouraged before costs are discussed. Based on tryout performance and fit, the coach will make a recommendation on team placement and any associated costs.
